# How to post these 76 listings (version 2)

Your own photos are edited only the way a phone editor would: crop, rotate, brightness, contrast, color balance and light sharpening. Nothing is AI-generated. Each `LISTING.txt` lists the edits made to each photo.

**Manufacturer images (44 listings).** Per your permission, 44 listings include one manufacturer product image from your package. It's the **last photo** when your cover already shows the product. It's **photo 2** when your cover is still a box, so buyers see the product right away. It is never the cover. Each description says which photo it is. I left it out of 086 (the image has "for representation only" printed on it, and the contents are unchecked) and 097 (the image looks gold, but the item is satin nickel). Several manufacturer images are small (265–600 px), so they may look a little soft.

## What's in each folder
- **`01.jpg`:** the **square cover**. Marketplace shows every listing as a square thumbnail in search and the feed, and crops anything that isn't square. I cropped each cover around the product, so the thumbnail shows the item and not the floor. Long items (towel bars, drains, slide bars) got a plain border instead of losing their ends.
- **`02.jpg`, `03.jpg`, …:** the rest of your photos, in upload order. They're brightened and color-corrected but not cropped, because buyers see them full-size.
- **`LISTING.txt`:**
  - Title, price, category, condition and description, ready to copy.
  - Notes for you, not to paste: shipping on/off, price checks, what to verify.
  - The photo edit log.
- **`INDEX.csv`:** one row per listing with the group, price, shipping setting and folder.

## Shipping settings (in each LISTING.txt)
- **Ship + local (58):**
  - Turn shipping on and keep local pickup on.
  - Weigh the packed box before you pick the label weight. If you enter less than it really weighs, the carrier's extra charge comes out of your payout.
  - Ship within 7 days.
- **Local only (8):**
  - 065 and 070 are over the $500 limit for shipped items.
  - 049, 050, 051 and 077 are 48–60 inches long.
  - 085 and 086 are mirrored cabinets.
- **Bundle only (10):** items under $40. On its own, the 10% fee plus a label costs more than the item.

Meta's rules (Marketplace Seller Protection Policy): a 10% selling fee on shipped orders (item + shipping + tax, $0.80 minimum), a $500 maximum price for shipped items, and a 7-day ship window. Seller Protection needs Meta's prepaid label.

## Posting routine
1. **Day 1:** folder 1 (7 listings). Edit the existing listing if you want to keep its link, or delete and repost. They're about a week old, so either is low-risk.
2. **Days 2–4:** folder 2, 10–15 a day. Don't post all 76 at once.
3. **As you reshoot:** folder 3. For the new photo, put the product out of the box on white poster board near a window. Use the grid on your phone camera to keep it level, and fill most of the frame.
4. **After 7 days:** tap **Renew** on listings with no messages.
5. **After 10–14 days** with no messages, lower the price about 10%. Change one thing at a time so you can tell what worked.
6. **Answer messages within the hour when you can.** Buyers often message several sellers at once. (Practitioner)

## Want to do the edits yourself on your phone?
The same edits work in the iPhone or Android photo editor: **Crop → Square (1:1)**, drag so the product fills about 80% of the frame, then **Brightness +10 to +20** and **Contrast +5 to +10**. Don't use filters, and don't change the color of gold, bronze or brass finishes, so buyers see the true finish.
