# Facebook Marketplace listing standard for Luxury Plumbing Surplus

Researched September 24, 2026. Each rule below is tagged with how well it is supported:

- **[Policy]**: Meta's own published rules.
- **[Platform guidance]**: official seller guidance from a large marketplace (eBay). It doesn't govern Facebook, but it shows what the platforms measure.
- **[Practitioner]**: reseller blogs and tests. These are useful but not independently verified.
- **[From your listings]**: what I found in your own photos and data.

## The standard

### Photos
1. **Photo 1 shows the product itself**, out of the box or clearly visible, centered, filling most of the frame. Don't lead with the box. [Platform guidance: eBay says "Your first photo appears in search results. Make it your best shot, with your item centered and fully visible."] [From your listings: 67 of 75 current listings lead with a box, carton or packaging.]
2. **Use a plain background and soft daylight.** Use white or light gray poster board near a window. No garage floor, rug, shoes or hands in frame. Use a darker backdrop only for chrome or polished items that disappear on white. [Platform guidance: eBay recommends "an uncluttered space, against a white/neutral background," and "Avoid using flash or adding filters."]
3. **Use photos of the actual item, not manufacturer stock images.** Your own inventory file marks the reuse rights for the manufacturer images as "unknown." Meta prohibits commerce content that infringes copyright. [Policy: Meta Commerce Policies, Third-Party Infringement] Practitioner sources also say stock images reduce trust and can get used-goods listings pulled. [Practitioner]
4. **Order the photos: product → finish close-up → everything included → box label with model number → connections.** The label shot lets contractors verify the exact SKU. [Platform guidance: eBay says to show "tags, labels and other key details."]
5. **Use 3 to 6 photos.** Don't add text, logos or watermarks, and remove duplicates. [Platform guidance: eBay says not to add text or watermarks.] [From your listings: two listings, 065 and 080, contain the same photo twice.]
6. **Upload at a high resolution** (these packages are 2,048 px on the long side). [Platform guidance: eBay recommends at least 1,600 × 1,600.]

### Title
7. **Order: Brand + collection + product type + finish, then "New," then the model number if it fits.** Example: `Rohl Myrina Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet, Matte Black - New`. Buyers search by brand and product type, and contractors search by model number. [Practitioner: descriptive titles with brand and condition get more views.] [From your listings: current titles are all-caps brand + model, often with no finish and no "new."]
8. **Keep it to about 70 characters or fewer.** Only the start of the title shows in the feed. [Practitioner]

### Description
9. **The first line says what it is: new, brand, product, finish.** Then give the model number, key specs (GPM, size, hole count), **what is and isn't included**, and the quantity. [Policy: Meta prohibits misleading commerce content.] [From your listings: none of the 75 Facebook descriptions state the finish, and several said "Includes: Complete…" even though your inventory file shows contents have not been checked (`contents_checked: false`).]
10. **Say plainly when it's trim only, body only, grate only or a part.** Name the rough valve it needs when you know it. This is the #1 cause of returns and no-shows for plumbing. [From your listings: your inventory notes already flag these; they now appear in the listing text.]
11. **Quote retail only when you can name the source and date** (for example, "Retail at Moen: $2,908.68, checked Sep 11, 2026"). Where the only source is your internal pricing workbook, I left the retail line out.
12. **Leave out clickable URLs.** Put your website on your Marketplace profile instead. [Policy: Meta prohibits "commerce content that contains deceptive links." Your links aren't deceptive.] [Practitioner: several sellers report that links in listings lower reach or trigger review. This is not confirmed by Meta.] [From your listings: every current description has two website URLs, and two listings (042, 048) are stuck "In review."]
13. **End with pickup/shipping, offers and a reference number** so you can match messages to stock.

### Price
14. **Keep your current prices** (about 50% of retail for fixtures) for the rebuild, so you can tell whether photos and copy were the problem. Change one variable at a time. If a listing gets no messages in 10–14 days, lower it about 10%. [Practitioner: sellers commonly price 10–20% under local comparable listings. My estimate for the timing.]

### Posting and pacing
15. **Don't delete and repost all 75 in one day.** Post 10–15 a day. [Practitioner: rapid bulk posting and reposting every 1–2 days reportedly reduced visibility; refreshing every 7–10 days is the common advice.]
16. **Delete and rebuild vs. edit in place.** Facebook lets you edit photos, title, price and description on a live listing. A new listing gets a new ID and a fresh "just listed" spot. [Practitioner] Your listings are only about a week old and have little history to lose, so rebuilding is low-risk. Where the cover photo is already good, editing in place is enough. **Renew**, available once a listing is 7+ days old, is a lighter refresh. [Practitioner]
17. **Boosting is optional.** Boosted listings reuse your listing's photos, title and description, and reach people within about 40 miles. Fix the photos before paying to boost them. [Policy/official: Meta Business Help, About Marketplace boosted listings]

### Common mistakes (all found in your current listings)
- A box or taped carton as the cover photo.
- Cluttered backgrounds: concrete floor, patterned rug, shoes, hands, other boxes.
- No finish in the title or description, although finish is the first thing a bath buyer filters on.
- "Complete" claims where contents weren't checked.
- Boilerplate text ("Questions are welcome", "Price: $X per item") that repeats the price field.
- Duplicate photos, and single-photo listings (034, 045, 046, 076, 102).
- Website URLs in every description.
