## Verdict
The new format is more accurate than the live listings. It shows the finish, gives the model on its own line, says "trim only" where needed, and drops unverified "complete" claims. As written, though, it will perform only a little better than today. Three problems cause most of the gap, and each takes minutes to fix:

1. **Shipping is never actually offered.** Every listing says "Ask whether shipping is available." Shipping has to be turned on in the listing's delivery settings. A line of text doesn't do it, and without it the listing is shown only to local buyers. [Practitioner]
2. **28 of 76 cover photos are a box, a label or a sealed carton.** The package already puts 27 listings on HOLD, which is correct. But 6 listings marked "improve" still lead with a box, although photo 02 shows the product (029, 040, 041, 063, 064, 118). Swap them.
3. **Every description ends with a clickable website URL.** Take it out and put the website on your profile. Two listings with URLs were stuck "In review." [Practitioner; not confirmed by Meta]

## Shipping: the rules that change the plan [Meta]
Source: Meta, Marketplace Seller Protection Policy / merchant policies, https://www.facebook.com/legal/merchant_policies

- **Selling fee on shipped orders: 10% of the whole transaction** (item plus shipping plus tax), with a $0.80 minimum. Local cash or pickup sales carry no fee.
- **Shipped items can't be listed above $500.** The policy calls $500 "the maximum list price for shipped items on Marketplace." That affects:
  - 065 Moen Weymouth ($1,455)
  - 070 MTI sink ($540)
  - 093 Rohl Graceline ($550)
  - 100 Rohl Lux ($540)

  These stay local only, or come down to $499 to allow shipping. 093 and 100 already show $499 on your website, so $499 settles the price conflict and makes them shippable.
- **Ship within 7 calendar days** or Meta may cancel the order.
- **Seller Protection requires Meta's prepaid label and on-site checkout.** It covers lost, stolen and late packages when you ship on time with tracking. You're responsible for entering the weight and dimensions correctly. If the scale is off, the carrier's charge for the difference comes out of your payout. [Practitioner: atoship]

## Line edits for every listing
Replace the last three lines of each description:

```
Gilbert, AZ pickup (by appointment) or shipped with tracking.
Opened for photos and inspection; never installed.
Message me with ref 118 for more photos or a bundle price.
```

- Only say "shipped" if you turned shipping on for that listing. For local-only items, say "Local pickup in Gilbert, AZ."
- "Opened for photos; never installed" is honest, because the photos show opened boxes. It also heads off "not as described" claims on shipped orders.
- Delete the `More details: https://...` line.

Other text fixes:
- **Add "New" to the title.** Buyers filter and scan for it.
- **Titles over 70 characters** (10 listings) need trimming. For example, 049 becomes `JACLO 60" Tile-In Shower Drain Grate, Brushed Stainless - New`.
- **Remove internal codes** from titles: "(bss)", "(pch)", "(15)", "(002)". For 085, "Beveled mirror" isn't a finish; put it in the description.
- **Fix brand spellings:** "Mti" → "MTI", "Qmdrain" → "QM Drain".
- **Add one plain-language search phrase** to each description, such as "shower valve trim kit," "linear shower drain," or "bathroom sink faucet." Facebook search matches words in the text, and most homeowners don't search model numbers. [Practitioner]

## Predicted performance [Estimate]
These are judgments from cover quality, product type and price, not data. Once rebuilt, check messages per listing per week.

- **Strong (7):** 053 JACLO faucet, 061 Moen rain head, 075 Phylrich faucet, 078 Riobel hand shower, 080 Riobel Paradox, 093 Rohl Graceline, 094 Rohl Myrina. Finished fixtures with the product in the cover photo. Post these first and boost them if you boost anything.
- **Good (4):** 035, 058, 062, 087.
- **Fair (30):** mostly trims and rough valves. They have a narrow local buyer pool, but shipping turned on should help them most.
- **Weak (6):** box or wrapped covers with no better photo available (030, 032, 033, 049, 065, 067). Reshoot these. 065 is your most expensive item, so reshoot it first.
- **Do not post yet (29):** HOLD and VERIFY items. Agree with the package: photograph the product and resolve the model or price first.

## Posting order
1. **Day 1:** the 7 "Strong" listings. Turn shipping on (except over-$500 items), set prices, and swap covers where noted.
2. **Days 2–4:** 10–15 a day from the Good and Fair groups.
3. **As reshoots finish:** the HOLD listings.
4. **After 7 days:** renew what hasn't had messages. After 10–14 days with none, lower the price about 10%.
5. **Photos:** upload the **full-resolution JPGs**, not the 480×640 WebP previews in this package. The folders in `listings/` from the earlier package have 2048 px JPGs of the same photos.
