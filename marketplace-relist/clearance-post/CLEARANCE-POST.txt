PRICE FIELD
Marketplace requires one price. Your options:
 a) The whole-lot price you'd actually take for everything (the strongest option; it tells buyers it's a real deal).
 b) A "starting at" price equal to your cheapest real item (for example $15), and say "prices vary, make an offer" in the text.
Avoid $0 or $1 bait pricing. Buyers find it annoying, and Meta's commerce rules prohibit misleading listings.

HOW TO SHOOT THE BOX PHOTO (it's the cover, so it decides the clicks)
- Stack the boxes like a store shelf, brand labels facing the camera. Put Rohl, Moen and Delta boxes in front.
- Put 3-5 of the best pieces out of the box on top or in front: the black Rohl faucet, the gold Riobel, a chrome showerhead.
  Real product in front of a wall of boxes reads as "big sale", not "pile of cardboard".
- Daylight, open garage door, no cars or clutter in frame. Take it from chest height so all the labels are readable.
- Shoot it square (1:1) or leave space around the stack. Marketplace crops the thumbnail to a square.
